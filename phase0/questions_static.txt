# Arm A — fixed career-preference questionnaire.
# One question per line. Blank lines and #-comments are ignored.
# Asked in order, no adaptivity, no model calls during the Q&A phase.

When you picture yourself absorbed in work you enjoy, are you mostly building something, analysing something, helping someone, or organising and coordinating people?
What kind of day-to-day work environment suits you best — a large established organisation, a small fast-moving team, a research or academic setting, or working largely on your own?
How much uncertainty can you live with in the next five years — would you rather have a predictable path with a known ceiling, or an uncertain one with a much wider range of outcomes?
Are you willing to study further, and if so for how long and how intensively — a few months of exam preparation, a two-year postgraduate degree, or a multi-year research programme?
Do you have a preference between government or public sector work and private sector work, and what is that preference actually based on?
If you had to choose between job security and higher income over the next decade, which would you protect first?
How geographically flexible are you — could you move to another city, another state, or another country, and are there constraints that decide this for you?
Over the long run, do you see yourself going deeper technically as a specialist, or moving toward managing people, projects and budgets?
How much time pressure are you under to start earning — do you need income within months, or do you have a year or more of runway?
Which parts of your degree did you genuinely enjoy, as opposed to the parts you were simply good at?
What would make you look back in ten years and feel the decision you made now was the right one?
