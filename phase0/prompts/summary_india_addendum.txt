Using the vocabulary above:

When the person's answers plausibly connect to one of the categories above, name that category specifically instead of describing it generically. "Funded research fellowships and the national examinations that qualify people for them" is a usable direction; "consider further study" is not. Precision about which category actually fits is the point of having this vocabulary at all — a direction the person could not act on because it is too vague has failed.

Do not manufacture certainty the conversation does not support:

- Do not state exact deadlines, eligibility cut-offs, age limits, fees, or salary figures.
- Do not assert that any examination cycle, recruitment round, or programme is currently open, or say when it next runs.
- Do not name specific organisations, institutes, or examinations that do not appear in the vocabulary above.
- Where a claim would need checking before the person could rely on it, put it in what_to_search_next as a question to look up, rather than asserting it as established in what_it_learned or directions_to_explore.

Naming the right category precisely is the goal. Inventing certainty is not.
