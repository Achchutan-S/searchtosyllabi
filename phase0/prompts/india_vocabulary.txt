Context vocabulary — the opportunity landscape in India:

The person is in India. What follows is a vocabulary of the kinds of organisations, qualifications, and entry routes that exist there. It is NOT a checklist to work through, NOT a decision tree to follow, and NOT a ranked list of recommendations. It exists so that what you produce is grounded in what actually exists rather than in generic abstraction — so that "government" is not one thing, "further study" is not one thing, and stability, examinations, and timelines all have real referents behind them.

- Central government: ministries and departments, the civil services and their examinations, staff selection routes, and the scientific and technical services that sit inside government.
- Public sector undertakings: engineering, energy, petroleum, telecom, heavy industry, mining, and transport enterprises — many of which recruit graduate engineers through national engineering entrance scores rather than running their own examinations.
- State government: state public service commissions, state-level technical and administrative posts, state utilities, boards, and corporations.
- Regulators and autonomous bodies: financial, securities, banking, insurance, telecom, energy, and standards or certification authorities.
- Defence and the strategic sector: the armed forces and their entry examinations, defence research and development establishments, ordnance and defence public sector units, and the space and atomic energy establishments.
- Research institutions: national laboratories and council-run institutes, institutes of national importance, and funded research fellowships together with the examinations that qualify people for them.
- Universities and academia: teaching and research posts in public and private institutions, and the qualifying examinations and doctoral routes that gate entry to them.
- Professional qualifications: chartered accountancy, cost accountancy, company secretaryship, actuarial science, law, and other examination-based professional bodies — several of which a graduate from any discipline can enter.
- Entrance and qualifying examinations as a category in their own right: national postgraduate engineering examinations, management admission tests, research fellowship examinations, civil services examinations, banking and insurance recruitment examinations, and teaching eligibility examinations.
- Banking and the financial sector: public sector bank recruitment, the central bank's own officer grades, insurance corporations, and the common recruitment processes that feed them.
- Private sector and beyond: product and services technology companies, startups, consulting, and routes to study or work abroad.
