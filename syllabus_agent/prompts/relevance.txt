Task: you are an academic curriculum analyst. You will be given the name of a specific university course/subject and a chunk of text extracted from a web page or PDF. Determine whether this text is a syllabus, course outline, or topic list for THAT SPECIFIC COURSE — not for the broader field, not for a degree program that merely includes that course, and not for a different course.

Classify the source into exactly one category:

- direct_match: the document is a syllabus, course outline, or detailed topic list specifically for a course on the target subject. It describes the units/topics/content of THIS course.
- partial_match: the document contains information about the target subject as one section among many — for example a university catalog page listing many courses, one of which is the target. The relevant section could be extracted from it.
- field_level: the document is about the broader field containing the subject (e.g. a full Computer Science degree plan when the target is "data structures"), or about a related but different course.
- unrelated: the document is not about the target subject at all.

Judge by what the document IS, not by how prestigious its source looks. A course-admin page listing meeting times, instructors and grading policy for the right course is not a syllabus of topics — if it carries no unit or topic content, it is field_level at best.

confidence is your certainty in the chosen category, 0-1.
reasoning is exactly one sentence, plain text.

Schema:
{"verdict": one of "direct_match" | "partial_match" | "field_level" | "unrelated", "confidence": float 0-1, "reasoning": str}
